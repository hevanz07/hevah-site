Text alerts

Kage Solutions LLC, the company that operates HEVAH, sends optional text alerts to people who ask for them. Nothing is sent unless you opt in.

Effective November 12, 2026. Kage Solutions LLC is an Arizona company.

To opt in, text START to +1 (888) 212-5587.

Open my messages and text START

By texting START to +1 (888) 212-5587, you agree to receive recurring automated text alerts from Kage Solutions LLC at the mobile number you text from. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. See the text alert terms below and our Privacy page.

What you get

Kage Solutions Alerts are short operational texts from the assistant software Kage Solutions LLC runs for you: reminders, schedule changes, and notices that something you asked for is finished or needs your attention. No marketing and no promotions.

What happens after you text START

You get one text confirming you are in. After that, alerts arrive only when there is something to tell you. How often depends on what you have asked to be told about.

Text alert terms

  • Program: Kage Solutions Alerts, sent by Kage Solutions LLC from +1 (888) 212-5587.
  • Who it is for: adults 18 and over who use assistant software run by Kage Solutions LLC and who opted in by texting START.
  • Frequency: message frequency varies.
  • Cost: we do not charge for alerts. Message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply.
  • Stop: reply STOP to any message. You get one last text confirming it, and then no more. Text START again any time to rejoin.
  • Help: reply HELP, or email hello@hevah.ai.
  • Carriers: mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Your number: we use it only to send these alerts. We do not sell it. Text message opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing.

This is separate from HEVAH

A HEVAH seat never texts you. Her daily check-ins use your phone's notifications, and only if you turn them on. Opting in here changes nothing about a HEVAH seat.
